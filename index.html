User Guide
How to Read the Ladder

This tool visualizes Bitcoin market structure using a combination of traditional moving averages and on-chain valuation levels.

The chart is designed to show where important support and resistance levels are located relative to the current BTC price.

Levels above the current BTC price are treated as resistances.

Levels below the current BTC price are treated as supports.

The tool does not make predictions. It is intended as a visual framework for understanding market positioning and historical valuation zones.

On-Chain Levels
Realized Price (RP)

Realized Price represents the average acquisition price of all BTC based on the price at which each coin last moved on-chain.

It is calculated as:

Realized Cap ÷ Circulating Supply

Realized Cap is calculated by valuing each BTC at the price when it last moved on-chain, rather than using the current market price.

Historically, Realized Price has acted as an important long-term support level during bear markets.

Short-Term Holder Cost Basis (STHCB)

STHCB estimates the average cost basis of short-term Bitcoin holders.

Short-term holders are typically defined as coins moved within the last ~155 days.

This level often reflects speculative market positioning and reacts relatively quickly to market changes.

Long-Term Holder Cost Basis (LTHCB)

LTHCB estimates the average cost basis of long-term Bitcoin holders.

Long-term holders are generally defined as coins held for more than ~155 days.

This metric tends to move slowly and is often associated with macro accumulation zones and strong-holder positioning.

True Market Mean (TMM)

True Market Mean is a valuation model derived from Cointime Economics.

It attempts to estimate a more economically weighted average market cost basis by adjusting for economically inactive supply.

TMM generally moves more slowly than spot price and may help identify macro market equilibrium zones.

Functions
Horizontal / Vertical View
Use the rotation button on the chart to switch between horizontal and vertical ladder layouts.
Auto Cluster
When Auto Cluster is enabled, nearby levels may be grouped into clusters to reduce visual overlap and improve readability. Cluster ranges are highlighted on the axis during hover. When Auto Cluster is disabled, levels are displayed individually.
Hover Intelligence
Hovering over a tag highlights its exact position on the axis. Hovering over a cluster tag highlights the entire cluster range. The tooltip displays the level name, current level value, and distance from current BTC price. Positive percentages indicate resistances above current BTC price. Negative percentages indicate supports below current BTC price.
Distance Filter
The distance filter limits which levels are displayed relative to the current BTC price. Available options are ±2%, ±5%, ±10%, and All. Smaller ranges focus on nearby market structure. All displays every available level regardless of distance from current BTC price.
