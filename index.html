User guide
How to read the chart

The vertical axis is a true price axis — position corresponds directly to price. The orange BTC line is your anchor. Levels above are resistances; below are supports.

Confluence zones are shaded bands where multiple independent signals converge. Hover to see each level's individual price.

When several levels (including possibly BTC) are too close on screen to display individually, they appear as a ×N badge. Hover to see all individual prices.

Green % = level above current price. Red % = below. Amber = within 0.8%.

Tag structure
X##MA — Moving Average
X = D (Daily) · W (Weekly) · M (Monthly)
## = 7 · 25 · 99 · 200 · 365

RP = Realized Price
STHCB = STH Cost Basis
LTHCB = LTH Cost Basis
TMM = True Market Mean
On-chain levels

Realized Price (RP) — Average acquisition price of all BTC. Key long-term support in bear markets.

STH Cost Basis (STHCB) — Cost basis of short-term holders (~155 days).

LTH Cost Basis (LTHCB) — Cost basis of long-term holders (155+ days).

True Market Mean (TMM) — Economically weighted cost basis from Cointime Economics.

Zone detection

Confluence zones use a mean-shift algorithm on the 1D price axis. Each level shifts toward its local density centroid, then levels with converged centers are grouped. Maximum zone width is 2%.

Display groups (×N badges) are separate — purely a readability feature when lines are within ~14px on screen. They carry no analytical meaning.
